# New Patient Guide

## Before Your First Visit

If this is your first visit to Sunrise Family Health Clinic, please have the following ready:

- A valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, driver's license, or passport)
- Your insurance card, if applicable
- Any referral letter from another doctor, if you were referred to us
- A list of any current medications you are taking
- Copies of any recent lab reports, prescriptions, or diagnostic reports relevant to your visit

If you have documents in physical or photo form, you can send them to our WhatsApp assistant ahead of your visit — this helps our staff prepare and speeds up your check-in.

## What Happens at Check-In

On arrival, please check in at the front desk with your ID. If you booked via WhatsApp, your appointment will already be in our system — you do not need to fill out a paper form unless it's your first-ever visit, in which case a short registration form will be provided.

## First-Time Registration

New patients will be asked to provide their name, date of birth, contact number, and address. This information is used to create your patient record and is kept confidentially in our system.

## Referral Patients

If you were referred to us by another doctor, please bring the original referral letter or send a photo of it through WhatsApp before your visit. This helps our specialists understand your case background ahead of time.

## Children and Pediatric Visits

For pediatric appointments (Dr. Rohan Verma), a parent or legal guardian must be present at check-in and during the consultation.

## What Our Assistant Can Help With

Our WhatsApp assistant can help you book, reschedule, or cancel appointments, answer general questions about clinic policies and hours, and accept document uploads ahead of your visit. It is not able to answer medical questions, interpret symptoms, or give treatment advice — those questions are always best directed to your doctor during your visit, or to our front desk for urgent concerns.
