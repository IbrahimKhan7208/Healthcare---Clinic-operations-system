# Appointment, Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

## Booking an Appointment

Appointments can be booked through our WhatsApp assistant, by calling the clinic directly, or in person at the front desk. New patients should be prepared to share their name and date of birth to help us locate or create their record.

All appointment requests made through the WhatsApp assistant are reviewed and confirmed by clinic staff before being finalized — this ensures the requested doctor and time slot are actually available.

## Rescheduling

Patients may reschedule an existing appointment at any time up to 4 hours before the scheduled visit, free of charge, through WhatsApp, phone, or the front desk. Rescheduling requests inside the 4-hour window may not be guaranteed and should be followed up with a phone call to confirm.

## Cancellations

- Cancellations made more than 24 hours in advance: no charge.
- Cancellations made within 24 hours: a nominal cancellation fee of ₹200 may apply.
- Repeated same-day cancellations may require future appointments to be confirmed with a deposit.

## No-Shows

Patients who do not show up for a scheduled appointment without cancelling will be marked as a no-show. Three or more no-shows within a 6-month period may require future bookings to be made only through the front desk rather than self-service channels.

## Late Arrivals

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled to protect other patients' appointment times, depending on the doctor's schedule that day.

## Follow-Up Appointments

If a doctor recommends a follow-up visit, our team will reach out via WhatsApp or phone to help schedule it. Follow-ups can also be requested directly by the patient at any time.
