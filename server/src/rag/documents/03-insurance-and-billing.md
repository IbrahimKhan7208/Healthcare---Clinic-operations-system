# Insurance & Billing

## Insurance Providers We Accept

Sunrise Family Health Clinic currently accepts the following insurance providers for cashless consultations:

- Star Health Insurance
- HDFC ERGO Health
- ICICI Lombard Health Care
- Niva Bupa Health Insurance

If your provider is not listed, you can still pay upfront and file for reimbursement with your insurer directly — our billing desk can provide the necessary receipts and diagnosis codes for this purpose.

## Consultation Fees (General Guide)

- General Physician consultation: ₹500
- Specialist consultation (Orthopedics, Dermatology, Pediatrics): ₹800
- Follow-up visit within 7 days of a prior consultation: ₹300

Fees for procedures, lab tests, or diagnostics are quoted separately at the time of the visit and are not covered by this general guide.

## Payment Methods

We accept cash, all major debit/credit cards, and UPI payments. Payment is collected at the front desk after your consultation, unless you are using a cashless insurance provider listed above.

## Insurance Verification

For cashless treatment, please share your insurance policy details and ID card at least one day before your appointment so our team can verify coverage in advance. Same-day cashless approval is not guaranteed if verification has not been completed beforehand.

## Billing Disputes

Any billing questions or disputes should be raised with our billing desk directly, either in person or by phone. Our WhatsApp assistant can help you understand general fee information but cannot resolve individual billing disputes or refunds.
