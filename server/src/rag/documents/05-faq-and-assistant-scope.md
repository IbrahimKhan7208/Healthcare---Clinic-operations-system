# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are accepted on a limited basis, subject to doctor availability that day. Booking an appointment in advance is strongly recommended to avoid long wait times.

## Can I get a prescription refilled without a visit?

Prescription refills for ongoing medication generally require a follow-up consultation, though minor refills may be approved by the treating doctor without an in-person visit at their discretion. Please message our assistant to request this and it will be forwarded to the relevant doctor's team for review.

## Do you offer telehealth/video consultations?

Currently, telehealth consultations are available only for existing patients with the General Physician (Dr. Priya Mehta), and only for follow-up visits, not first-time consultations.

## How do I get my lab reports?

Lab reports are typically shared via WhatsApp or email within 24–48 hours of your test, depending on the type of test. You can also collect a physical copy from the front desk.

## Can I change my registered doctor?

Yes — let our front desk or WhatsApp assistant know, and we can help transfer your ongoing care to a different doctor within the same department, subject to availability.

## What This Assistant Can and Cannot Help With

This assistant is designed to help with clinic administration only: booking, rescheduling, and cancelling appointments; answering questions about clinic hours, locations, insurance, billing, and policies; and accepting document uploads ahead of a visit.

This assistant **cannot** and **will not**:
- Diagnose symptoms or medical conditions
- Recommend treatments, medications, or dosages
- Interpret lab results or medical reports
- Provide any form of medical advice

If you ask a question in any of these categories, this assistant will let you know it cannot help with that specific question and will offer to connect you with clinic staff or recommend you discuss it directly with your doctor. For urgent medical concerns, please call the clinic directly or seek emergency care — do not rely on this assistant for anything urgent.
