# Sunrise Family Health Clinic — Hours & Locations

## About Us

Sunrise Family Health Clinic is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic serving patients across Mumbai. We offer general physician consultations, orthopedics, dermatology, and pediatrics, along with basic diagnostic and lab referral services.

## Our Locations

- Phone: +91-22-4567-8901
- Hours: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Closed Sundays.

- Address: Ground Floor, Sunrise Annexe, Bandra West, Mumbai
- Phone: +91-22-4567-8950
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

## Departments Available

Sunrise Family Health Clinic offers consultations in: General Medicine, Orthopedics, Dermatology, and Pediatrics.

For a specific doctor's name, department assignment, or current availability, please ask our assistant directly — this information is kept up to date in our live booking system rather than in this document, since doctor schedules change regularly.

## Public Holidays

The clinic remains closed on major national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, Christmas). Reduced hours may apply around other regional holidays — please confirm before visiting on a holiday.

## Parking

Limited parking is available at the Andheri branch (first-come, first-served). The Bandra branch does not have dedicated parking; a public paid lot is located two minutes' walk away.
